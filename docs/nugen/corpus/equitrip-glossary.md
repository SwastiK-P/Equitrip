# Equitrip domain notes — group travel and shared expenses, as Indian travellers talk about them

## What equitrip is
Equitrip is a group-trip planner and shared expense ledger. A trip has a title (like "Goa Getaway"), a destination, start and end dates, travellers, and bookings. Every booking is also a ledger entry: someone paid for it, and it is split between some of the travellers. The app works out who owes whom. Equi is the assistant inside the app. Equi reads questions and sorts them; the app itself works out every figure. Equi never makes up an amount, a date or a name.

## Trip phases
upcoming: the trip has not started. under way now (live): today falls between its start and end dates. finished (past): it has ended.
"This trip", "the trip", "is trip", "abhi wali trip" mean the trip being discussed, or the one under way.
"My next trip", "agli trip", "upcoming trip" mean the next one that has not started.
"My last trip", "pichli trip", "previous trip", "last wali trip" mean the most recent finished trip.
"All my trips", "saari trips", "meri trips" mean every trip.
A place name that is a trip's destination means that trip: "Goa" means "Goa Getaway" when that trip is to Goa.

## Booking kinds
flight: the thing that flies. Identified by airline code and number: 6E 2134 (IndiGo), AI 864 (Air India), UK 955 (Vistara), QP 1102 (Akasa Air), SG 8157 (SpiceJet), EK 501 (Emirates).
train: identified by number and name: 12951 Rajdhani Express, 22439 Vande Bharat, 12009 Shatabdi Express. Indian Railways terms: PNR (booking reference), 1A/2A/3A (AC sleeper classes), SL (sleeper), CC (chair car), CNF (confirmed), RAC (reservation against cancellation, a shared berth), WL (waitlisted), Tatkal (short-notice quota), IRCTC (the booking site).
transfer: getting between places on the ground or water: airport transfer, airport pickup, airport drop, cab, taxi, Ola, Uber, auto, station drop, ferry, scooter or bike rental, self-drive car. An airport transfer is a transfer, never a flight.
stay: where people sleep: hotel, resort, villa, homestay, hostel (Zostel), houseboat, tent, Airbnb, OYO. Check-in and check-out belong to the stay. Checking in at an airport does not.
activity: things to do: tours, treks, rafting, paragliding, scuba, snorkelling, safaris, museum and fort visits, temple visits, cruises, aarti.
meal (food): breakfast, lunch, dinner, restaurant bookings, cafes, street food, drinks. Hotel breakfast is a meal, not a stay.
other: anything else: free time, rest day, visa appointment, currency exchange, travel insurance, shopping time.

## Merchants and what they usually are
Swiggy, Zomato, Swiggy Instamart, Blinkit, EatSure, Domino's, restaurants, cafes, dhabas: food.
Ola, Uber, Rapido, BluSmart, Namma Yatri, auto-rickshaw, taxi, cab, Zoomcar, Royal Brothers: transfer.
IndiGo, Air India, Akasa Air, SpiceJet, Vistara, Emirates, flight bookings on MakeMyTrip, Cleartrip, EaseMyTrip, ixigo: flight.
IRCTC, ConfirmTkt, RailYatri, trains: train.
OYO, Treebo, FabHotels, Zostel, Airbnb, Booking.com, Agoda, Taj, Leela, Oberoi, Lemon Tree: stay.
Thrillophilia, Klook, Viator, GetYourGuide, BookMyShow, entry tickets: activity.
A booking site sells many kinds; the item decides, not the site: a MakeMyTrip hotel voucher is a stay.

HOW PEOPLE ASK ABOUT MONEY (HINGLISH)
dena / dena hai: to pay, to owe. "Rahul ko kitna dena hai?" = how much do I owe Rahul (balance).
lena / lena hai: to receive, to be owed. "Priya se kitna lena hai?" = how much does Priya owe me (balance).
hisaab: the account, the reckoning. "Hisaab kitna baaki hai?" = what is still unsettled (balance).
baaki: remaining, pending. udhaar: a debt, money lent.
banta hai: comes to, is due. "Mera kitna banta hai?" = what is my share or my balance (balance).
settle karna: to settle up (balance).
kharcha / kharch: spending. "Total kitna kharcha hua?" = how much was spent (spending).
laga / gaya: cost, went. "Hotel ka kitna laga?" = how much did the hotel cost (spending).
mehenga: expensive. sasta: cheap. "Sabse mehenga kya tha?" = what was the most expensive thing (spending).
bhara / pay kiya: paid. "Bill kisne bhara?" = who paid the bill (people).
kaun kaun: who all. "Kaun kaun aa raha hai?" = who is coming (people).
aaj: today. kal: tomorrow (for plans ahead) or yesterday (for things past); for "kal ka plan" it means tomorrow.
subah: morning. shaam: evening. raat: night. kitne baje: at what time.
khana: food. ruk rahe: staying. ghoomna: to go around, sightsee. mausam: weather. pack karu: should I pack.

BALANCE, SPENDING AND PEOPLE ARE DIFFERENT QUESTIONS
balance is who owes whom: owe, owed, settle, get back, even, dena, lena, hisaab, udhaar.
spending is what things cost: spent, cost, total, damage, budget, expensive, kharcha, laga, mehenga.
people is who is on the trip and who paid for something: who paid, who's coming, how much has Rahul paid, kisne bhara.
"How much do I owe Rahul?" is balance with person Rahul. "How much has Rahul paid?" is people with person Rahul. "How much did food cost?" is spending with category food.

## Splitting
Expenses can be split equally, by exact amounts, by shares, or by percentage, among some or all travellers. A settlement is a payment from one traveller to another to clear a balance, often over UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM). A settlement stays pending until the person receiving it confirms it.

## Times in indian itineraries
Times are written as 14:30, 2:30 PM, 1430 hrs or 14.30; all mean 14:30. A row without a time has no time; a stay usually has none. IST is India Standard Time (UTC+5:30).

## Currency
₹, Rs, Rs. and INR all mean Indian rupees. Amounts are written with Indian grouping: ₹1,25,000 is one lakh twenty-five thousand rupees. A lakh is 100,000; a crore is 10,000,000. Prices are copied from documents, never estimated.
